Smart Gas Meter Guide

Use the actual gas meter, not the in-home display. For gas rating, keep the decimal digits because you need the small change in volume.

A + B buttons

Press A to wake the display. Press A again until METER INDEX appears with a reading followed by m³. Record the full displayed value including decimals for gas rating.

Three red buttons — A, B, C

Press the left/A button to wake the meter. The volume reading is normally displayed in m³. A/B scroll and C commonly selects menu items.

Numeric keypad

Press 9. Wait for VOLUME, then the number followed by m³. Record the full value including decimals for gas rating.

Circle + two arrows

Press the middle circle button. The gas volume reading should appear followed by m³.

Orange + blue arrow buttons

Press the orange button until a number followed by m³ is displayed.

EDMI — two white buttons

Press the right-hand button (often OK/arrow). Record the volume shown in m³.

G4 / GWi — buttons either side

Press the right-hand button to wake the display, then press it until the m³ volume is shown. Ignore a small screen identifier such as 01/02, but keep the meter reading decimals for gas rating.

Engineer note: Supplier guides tell customers to ignore decimals when submitting billing readings. For a gas-rate test, do the opposite: use the changing decimal digits so the measured volume over the timed period is captured.
